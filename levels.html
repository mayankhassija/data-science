Data Science
Syllabus Levels

Course progression

Levels

One ordered path. Each level builds on the capabilities developed in the previous level.

LEVEL 01

Computing & Python

Build genuine programming ability: reading code, writing code, debugging, solving problems, and structuring programs.

LEVEL 02

Working With Data

Learn to take real-world data and turn it into something usable: inspect, clean, transform, explore, visualize, and explain.

LEVEL 03

Data & Statistical Reasoning

Learn how data is stored, queried, measured, sampled, compared, and interpreted.

LEVEL 04

Machine Learning

Move from describing data to building systems that learn patterns from data: formulate problems, train models, validate them, evaluate them, and iterate.

LEVEL 05

Applied Data Science

Work on increasingly unfamiliar and ambiguous problems. Learn to decide what problem matters, what data matters, what method is appropriate, and whether the result is useful.

LEVEL 06

Internship Readiness

Integrate the skills developed throughout the course through realistic projects, reproducible work, technical communication, independent problem-solving, and unfamiliar assessments.
