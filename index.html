How it works

Follow the path

The curriculum is deliberately ordered. You do not need to decide what technology to learn next.

Learn in short stages

The curriculum is divided into levels and roughly 15-minute stages. Each stage is designed around active learning.

Do the work

Exercises, problem sets and projects turn knowledge into something you can actually demonstrate.

Progress by competence

Completing videos is not the goal. Demonstrating that you can use what you learned is.
