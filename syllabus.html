Data Science

A single, ordered path from foundational programming knowledge toward Data Science internship readiness.

Why this exists

There are thousands of tutorials, courses and technologies to choose from. More information does not solve the problem of knowing what to learn, in what order, or whether you can actually use what you learned.

This course removes that choice. The curriculum provides one deliberate progression, with practical work throughout.

Who it is for

The starting learner has basic programming exposure but does not yet have the ability to independently solve real data problems or produce professional Data Science work.

The learning unit

The curriculum is divided into levels. Each level contains stages that are designed to take roughly 15 minutes.

A stage may contain an explanation, an external lecture or video, a worked example, an exercise, a coding task, or a short challenge. The medium changes according to what is most effective for the concept.

The standard

Watching material is not considered mastery. The learner must repeatedly demonstrate that they can apply ideas to unfamiliar problems.

Curriculum status

The curriculum is being built and tested before release. Its sequence will be based on prerequisite relationships, internship requirements, practical usefulness and the broader technology landscape.
