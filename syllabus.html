Data Science

A structured path for aspiring Data Science interns.

Why this course exists

There is no shortage of tutorials, courses, and information about data science. The problem is knowing what to learn, in what order, and whether you can actually do anything useful with what you have learned.

This course provides one deliberate path through the technologies, concepts, and practical work required to become a strong candidate for Data Science internships.

Who this is for

This course is designed for people with basic programming knowledge who want to move beyond consuming tutorials and become capable of independently solving data problems and building useful things.

How the course works

The course follows a fixed sequence. Each stage builds on the previous one. Learners study concepts, apply them through problem sets, and progressively work on less-guided projects.

The standard

Watching the material is not considered completion. The goal is demonstrated ability: understanding problems, choosing appropriate approaches, working with real data, building solutions, and explaining the decisions behind them.

The curriculum is designed to be revised as the technology and expectations of the industry change.
